Strategy Guide

Phase 1: Exploration — Place many small bets ($1-5) across different categories and signal types. The engine tracks which strategies produce positive returns.

Phase 2: Identification — Once a strategy shows consistent edge over 20+ bets with a win rate above 55%, it gets flagged for scaling.

Phase 3: Scaling — Increase bet sizes on proven strategies while continuing small exploratory bets to find new edges.
